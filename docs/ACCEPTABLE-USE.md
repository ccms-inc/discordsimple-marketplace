   you post through the Service, **you are the publisher** and are responsible
   for the content and its compliance with applicable law.
3. **Abuse mentions or harass.** Attempt to circumvent the Service's
   mass-mention safeguards (`@everyone`, `@here`, and role pings are disabled),
   or use the Service to harass, dogpile, or target individuals or communities.
4. **Violate Discord's rules.** Breach the
   [Discord Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms),
   [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines), developer/platform
   policies, or the rules of any server you post to — or the terms of Anthropic
   or any other connected service.
5. **Post unlawful or harmful content.** Transmit content that is illegal,
   fraudulent, defamatory, infringing, harassing, or that contains malware,
   phishing links, or other malicious payloads.
6. **Infringe or misappropriate** intellectual property or privacy rights, or
   post others' personal or confidential data without authorization.
7. **Abuse or attack the Service.** Circumvent authentication, rate limits,
   plan limits, or tenant isolation; probe or scan the infrastructure; attempt
   to access other accounts' servers, channels, or data; or interfere with or
   disrupt the Service (including denial of service).
8. **Exceed plan limits or automate abusively.** Use the Service beyond your
   plan's entitlements (servers, channels, monthly posts) or generate abusive
   automated volume.
9. **Misrepresent identity.** Impersonate any person, bot, or organization, or
   present Service posts as coming from someone who did not authorize them.
